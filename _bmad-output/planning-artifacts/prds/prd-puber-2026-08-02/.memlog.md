- [2026-08-07] decision: The question did expose a real ambiguity in the FR-20/FR-29 overlap — FR-29 said a stale driver "is treated as unavailable" without saying whether that MUTATES declared status or is derived. Consequence if it mutates: a driver passing through a tunnel silently ends their shift and receives nothing until they notice and re-tap. Resolved by separating the two facts: status (FR-20) is declared intent, written only by driver action or ride lifecycle; reachability (FR-29) is derived from heartbeat freshness and never writes to status. Matchable = declared AVAILABLE AND reachable. A driver who loses and regains signal recovers automatically on the next heartbeat — "a tunnel does not end a shift, and only the driver ends a shift."
